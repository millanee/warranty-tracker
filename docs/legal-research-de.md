# Legal Research – Germany

## Scope

The first version of Warranty Tracker is designed for consumers in Germany
who purchase physical goods from commercial sellers.

The app provides general consumer information only. It does not provide
individual legal advice.

## Statutory defect rights

For most physical goods, statutory claims for defects generally expire two
years after delivery of the product.

The relevant starting date is the delivery date, not necessarily the order
date or payment date.

If the user does not enter a delivery date, the app may use the purchase date
as an estimated fallback date. The interface must clearly identify the result
as an estimated expiration date.

## Used goods

For used goods, the statutory period can under certain conditions be reduced
to one year.

The app must not automatically assume that every used product has a one-year
period. The user must be able to select whether a valid reduction was agreed.

Default for used goods:

- No reduction selected: 24 months
- Reduction explicitly selected: 12 months

## Burden of proof

If a defect becomes apparent within one year after the transfer of risk, it is
generally presumed in a consumer purchase that the product was already defective
at that time, unless this presumption is incompatible with the product or defect.

This rule may be explained in the information section but does not affect the
expiration-date calculation.

## Manufacturer warranty

A manufacturer or commercial warranty is voluntary and separate from statutory
defect rights.

The app must therefore not automatically assume that every product has a
one-year or two-year manufacturer warranty.

The user should select:

- No manufacturer warranty
- Known warranty duration
- Known warranty expiration date

## Product decisions

The product model should support:

- Purchase date
- Optional delivery date
- New or used condition
- Optional agreed reduction for used goods
- Optional manufacturer warranty
- Warranty duration or exact expiration date
- Calculated statutory expiration date
- Calculated manufacturer warranty expiration date

## Disclaimer

Warranty Tracker provides general organizational tools and consumer information.
Calculated dates are estimates and do not replace examination of the purchase
contract, warranty terms or individual legal advice.
