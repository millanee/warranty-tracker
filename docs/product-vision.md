# Product Vision

## Working Title

Warranty & Statutory Rights Tracker

## Vision

The app helps consumers organize purchase receipts, track warranty and statutory
rights periods, and avoid missing potential repair or replacement claims.

## Problem

Many consumers lose purchase receipts or cannot find them when a purchased
product becomes defective.

In addition, many consumers do not understand the difference between:

- a voluntary manufacturer warranty, and
- statutory consumer rights provided by German law.

As a result, defective products may be replaced or discarded even though the
consumer may still have a valid claim.

## Solution

The app allows users to:

- register purchased products,
- store photos of receipts,
- calculate relevant warranty and statutory rights dates,
- receive reminders before important deadlines,
- quickly retrieve purchase information and receipts,
- access general consumer information,
- follow a simple complaint checklist when a product becomes defective.

## Target Market

The initial target market is Germany.

The first version is intended for price-conscious consumers who want to organize
receipts for electronics, household appliances, and other valuable purchases.

## Value Proposition

Users can register a purchase in a short amount of time and later find the
product information, receipt, relevant dates, and general guidance in one place.

## Product Principles

- Simplicity over unnecessary complexity
- Local storage over cloud dependency
- Privacy by design
- Clear consumer information
- Small and testable product increments
- No legal advice
- No functionality outside the active product scope
- German user interface
- English development artifacts and source code

## Language Strategy

All development-related content must be written in English, including:

- source code,
- variable and class names,
- documentation,
- GitHub issues,
- user stories,
- acceptance criteria,
- tests,
- commit messages,
- pull requests,
- Cursor Rules,
- Cursor Skills,
- Cursor Commands,
- AI prompts.

All user-facing content in the application must be written in German because
the MVP targets the German market.

## MVP Validation

The MVP is considered useful if test users can:

1. register a product without assistance,
2. retrieve the stored receipt later,
3. understand the displayed warranty information,
4. understand that manufacturer warranties and statutory rights are different,
5. indicate that they would use the app for future purchases.
